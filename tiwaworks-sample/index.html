About TiwaWorks

20+ years of building, producing and connecting.

TiwaWorks is an Atlanta-based integrated services firm with experience across events, marketing, communications, digital programs, community engagement and business development.

Founded in 2004. Built from entrepreneurial roots into a multi-market services company.

Execution-focused. Comfortable managing complex timelines, vendors, audiences and high-visibility deliverables.

Community-aware. Deep experience reaching diverse audiences in the U.S. and internationally.

Leadership

Tiwa Williams

Founder, TiwaWorks

Tiwa Williams founded TiwaWorks while attending Georgia Southwestern State University and built the company through event production, marketing, partnership development and community engagement.

Georgia Southwestern later recognized Williams with its Visionary Award for entrepreneurial achievement and the growth of TiwaWorks.
