Leadership

Tiwa Williams

Founder • Entrepreneur • Producer • Connector

Tiwa Williams founded TiwaWorks while attending Georgia Southwestern State University and grew the company into an international marketing and event-planning business spanning multiple markets.

His approach is rooted in relationship building, community, partnership development and hands-on execution. That foundation powers TiwaWorks' work across major events, marketing programs, business initiatives and digital solutions.

“Handshake by handshake” reflects the relationship-first approach behind TiwaWorks' growth.

Georgia Southwestern recognized Williams with its Visionary Award, honoring his entrepreneurial work and the growth of TiwaWorks.
